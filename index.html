Controls

WASD or arrows to walk, swim or drive
Shift or Boost for a car burst
On sports grounds: hold J or the shot button,
then release to kick or shoot
Space to jump or hop a boat
Shift to run or swim faster
Space / Shift to climb / descend in a plane
+ / − to zoom · 0 resets framing
Pinch or scroll also zooms
Q / E to rotate the view
F to sit / stand or enter / exit a vehicle
T to talk to a nearby person
M for nearby activities · I for your bag
In activities: arrows or WASD select, Enter acts
1–4 or Q/E change section; Tab reaches every action
Escape closes actions, then pauses
B to buy · V to sell · H to work / collect / deliver · G to eat
Y to share food with someone while seated
R to return to the start
