Controls

WASD or arrows to walk, swim or drive

then release to kick or shoot
Space to jump or hop a boat
Shift to run or swim faster
Space / Shift to climb / descend in a plane
Pinch or scroll to zoom
Q / E to rotate the view
F to sit / stand or enter / exit a vehicle
T to talk to a nearby person
B to buy · V to sell · H to work / collect / deliver · G to eat
