Vehicle controls

Press F to enter, then use WASD. Space jumps on foot, bicycles and skateboards, or hops a boat. Shift runs or swims faster. Space climbs and Shift descends in the plane. F exits to dry ground when available, otherwise into water. Land before exiting.
