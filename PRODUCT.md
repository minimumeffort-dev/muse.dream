# Dreams Lab

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Researchers who want to define a world, context, scenario and task, run an AI
agent in that environment, change parameters and compare measured outcomes.

## Product Purpose

Muse interprets the research brief and proposes Dream source. The host validates
that source, executes supported rules and measures outcomes independently of the
agent. Researchers can inspect the generated source and replay a recorded run.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The Lab is the sole product at the repository root. It selectively reuses the
Dream framework and the approved Workspace A interface, with compact controls
and contextual results. The human-facing flow is scenario, conditions, run and
outcome. Advanced settings and Dream rules stay behind a disclosure; results and
comparisons explain measured outcomes before technical evidence.
Workspace now uses local copies of the compiler, logical episode engine,
recording format and selected Three.js rendering modules. Run, pause, step,
history seeking, direct source editing and JSONL export work. The 3D buildings,
walking agents, carried purchases and service gestures observe accepted state.
The local builder expands three explicit briefs: delivery, warehouse restocking
and classroom preparation, with bounded parameters. Validated Dream presentation
selects buildings, appearances and cargo independently of scenario IDs. Baseline
agents are deterministic mocks. A separate local HTTP host accepts actor-scoped
decisions, commits joint steps and continues when its viewer closes. One external
mock process proves that boundary. Live and recorded results link findings to exact
recorded transitions. Saved experiments have immutable revisions and per-run
source/configuration pins. Researchers create runs from the UI, reopen history
after a host restart, explicitly resume interrupted runs and compare two runs
with links into their evidence. No Muse calls, physical navigation, training or
domain-validity claims are implemented.

The compiler and deterministic runner are copied into the Lab with pinned
provenance. Selected Dream assets, fonts, package/config files, scripts and tests
are local too. The app imports no source or build assets outside the repository.
The old game has been retired; reusable object definitions remain in the local asset library.

## Brand Commitments

Use Minimum Effort's established identity: Manrope body type, Archivo headings,
warm white, charcoal and yellow. Keep the interface compact, readable and
keyboard accessible. Use concrete language and no invented performance claims.

## Open Decisions

The design chooser recorded Workspace as the initial selection. Real Muse
integration, hosted run persistence, first physical scenario, observation
profiles, inference budget and cloud storage remain later decisions.
