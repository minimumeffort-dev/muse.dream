# Direct vehicle control

Verified locally on 2026-10-16 against the source fingerprints in `source.sha256`.

The old bicycle, skateboard, boat and plane rides ignored player input and followed routes. Dream 2 now declares an optional control profile on the seat. Those four live vehicles start parked, take direct input while occupied and retain their positions when vacated. The original racing car remains the initial vehicle. F exits to dry accessible ground; WASD walks; F takes over a nearby seat. The autonomous train retains its legacy passenger seat.

The active body owns its pose. Unoccupied vehicle records store only parked poses, including the original car. Collision, rendering, particles and the map read those same poses. The accepted scene stays immutable. Ground vehicles reuse the fixed-step car simulation with their own tuning, radius and height. Walking cannot trigger racing boosts or fractures. Boat support checks the visible hull against water/banks/decks, separately from cylinder collision. Aircraft support takeoff, climb, steering/bank, stall descent, landing and recovery after a water landing. Plane exits leave space for the airframe, and an occupied vehicle is excluded from scenery fading. The standing Explorer is authored in Dream 2 and is reused on the skateboard; other seats use the existing traveler at its independent size.
