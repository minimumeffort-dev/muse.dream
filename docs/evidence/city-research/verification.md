# River city verification, r26

16 October 2026. The live layout is replaced, including the old ring and starting area. Sources remain handmade mock Dream 2. No provider calls, generated code, imported meshes or traffic solver were added.

## Source and acceptance

The accepted scene is 14000 × 11000 units: 1.4 × 1.1 km at ten units per metre. It contains 70 reusable assemblies, 1,192 source parts, 511 placements, 30 streets, 54 crossing declarations, 211 entrance/platform sites, nine routes, three stone bridges and one continuous river/lake. There are 187 homes and shops across four areas. The source stays inside the existing animation, NPC, reactive-object, fragment and project budgets.

`junction` references share exact coordinates between connected streets, the road and bridges. The map is generated by `node scripts/render-city-map.mjs` from the fully accepted Scene, including actual building footprints and entrance paths. It is not a separate proposed layout. The [Delft study](delft-summary.json) records the external data source, method, attribution and checksum. [City design](../../city-design.md) states exactly which book descriptions and authorized excerpts were available; full books were not read.

The new live-world checks cover all 211 sites in one pedestrian component, full entrance-path collision clearance, road crossings only at declared zebras, named destinations reachable from stops, shared endpoints, full bridge cross-sections, and a pilot completing all eight ordered checkpoints. ICE and BOOST have full road clearance. A launch crosses below the market bridge and can disembark onto dry ground. Architecture checks use transformed primitive bounds for attachment and roof support and sample the full wall footprint against collision bodies. Shelter posts, rear panels and the overhead roof have separate finite contacts.

The frozen r24 scene in `tests/fixtures/legacy-world.json` preserves older timber-bridge, physics, vehicle, destruction and terrain regression cases. It does not constrain the replacement world. Current-world tests separately cover the new layout, clear driving starts, breakable market props and boat navigation.

## Limits

Buildings are stylized primitive assemblies with conservative cylinder contacts and no interiors. Transit follows bounded authored routes and waits; it does not negotiate traffic. NPC journeys and sports are mock routines, with no school, hospital, economy, match or employment simulation. Ground vehicles retain one height per XY, while boats and swimmers use the validated water layer beneath bridges. Arbitrary stacked roads and generated excavation remain unsupported.

The full world remains resident with existing instancing, geometry tiles and frustum culling. Pixel ratio, shadow resolution, post-processing, particles, render distance and asset detail were not reduced. Local timings cannot certify performance on a less powerful laptop.

## Local rendering measurements

The native in-app browser ran 60 warm-up frames plus 300 measured driving frames per location, without concurrent builds or tests. At the normal 1.5 pixel ratio (1345 × 1122 framebuffer), the dense center averaged 3.65 ms of CPU work and 0.99 ms GPU time, with a 16.67 ms mean animation-frame interval. The [raw default-quality town report](profile-town-default.json) preserves means, p95 values, draws and triangles. The matching [outskirts run](profile-outskirts-default.json) averaged 3.61 ms CPU, 0.97 ms GPU and 16.67 ms between animation frames at the same resolution. These are local measurements on this machine, not a less powerful laptop result or a cold-start measurement.

Matched diagnostic runs at DPR 1 and 897 × 748 are also retained for the [center](profile-town.json) and [outskirts](profile-outskirts.json). This override belongs only to the development profiler. The production pixel-ratio policy and other quality settings are unchanged.
