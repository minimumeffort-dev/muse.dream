VOXEL CONSTRUCTION
Use voxels id(material=mat,at=[x,y,z],size=[sx,sy,sz],grid=[nx,ny,nz],chunks=[cx,cy,cz]?){fill solid(from=[0,0,0],to=[nx,ny,nz]);cut opening(from=[...],to=[...]);}. Each voxels declaration is one rigid part. The block permits fill/cut, let and bounded repeat. shape=box is default; ellipsoid tests cell centers inside the from/to bounds. Operations run in order. Coordinates are integer half-open cell indices, 0 <= from < to <= grid; each axis 1..32, at most 32768 cells, 64 expanded operations, 8192 greedy surface quads. Assembly limits: 65536 cells, 1048576 fill/cut cell visits, existing 32 parts. Linked project: 1048576 cells across assembly definitions. Use equal size/grid ratios for cubic cells. Use separate volumes for materials. No raw voxel arrays, shaders, scripts or remote meshes.
Optional chunks must divide grid axes exactly into at most 8 nonempty pieces. Requires impact break or a break zone, no fragment wrapper. All pieces count toward existing 8 fragments per assembly, 24 reactive placements and 128 scene fragments. Unchunked volumes can use normal fragment groups/topple. Voxel parts disallow deform, cloth and part motion; use rigid joints for animation. Explicit body/contact cylinders remain the physics geometry: carved holes do not create traversable openings, editable terrain or tunnels. The live city uses the optional world construction policy below.

Construction: silhouette and measure first. Doors/window heights follow usable floors; glazing must remain visible through carved frames. Roofs meet walls, porch posts meet base/canopy, gutters meet leaders. Rolling tires/hubs stay coaxial, bogies support their shell, and rideable seats must fit an independently measured rider. Check transformed multi-angle renders, not only schemas. Checked live constructions: examples/terraced-house.dream, examples/city-bus.dream, examples/cabin-launch.dream.

WHOLE-WORLD VOXELS
For a complete world, put voxel_world(object_cell=1,ground_cell=8,height_step=1); beside world/road in the owner file. Exactly one declaration; object_cell .25..4 local units, ground_cell exactly 4 or 8, height_step .5..1. The compiler applies it to all linked templates, furnishings, player and flocks. Continue writing part/beam as compact shape stamps; the engine constructs their occupied cells, fits the authored envelope, and retains material/joint/fragment ownership. Explicit voxels/fill/cut remain exact and keep existing budgets. Ground and infrastructure use resident column tiles; paint keeps fine cells/layer separation and water keeps rigid column wave anchors. Fixed construction resolution, no camera LOD. Physics remains explicit contacts plus the smooth driving heightfield. No editable terrain, automatic destructibility or per-cell physics. Do not output voxelSize/voxelWorld compiler metadata in source.

SPORTS PRACTICE
sport id(kind=football|basketball,surface=surface_id,ball=placement_id,targets=[first,second],target_at=[x,y,z],target_size=[width,height]); connects existing world data after linking. At most 8 grounds, each with its own non-overlapping dry surface and distinct bounce ball. Ball is a static prop with a round knockable body: world radius .5..3, height twice radius. Use measure(kind=custom,axis=x,meters=.22/.24) to resolve real ball size from a local body(radius=4,height=8); do not lower legacy body bounds. Targets are two static upright assemblies at least 64 units apart; target_at and target_size use their local units and follow placement heading/scale. Football target is a grounded opening 6..120 wide and 4..50 high, local +Z points out of the field, opposing target normals. Basketball target is a clear X/Z ring diameter 2.5..12, fitting the ball, at height 16..50; local +Z points into the court. Target anchor must lie in the actual assembly bounds and within 32 of the surface. Surface sampled height variation <=3, no water. Existing object/reactive budgets still apply. Engine owns walk-up dribbling, charged shots, scoring, recovery and camera; no generated controller code, team AI, fouls or bank-shot backboard physics. Complete source: examples/sports-practice.dream.
